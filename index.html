About us

Experience of time

Hazyna Gurluşyk Economic Society was founded on 14 September 2009. Created on the basis of the National Program to transform the social and living conditions of the population, the company has gained deep experience in construction, electricity and water supply.

Our goal is to achieve economic excellence and create new employment opportunities for the citizens of Turkmenistan. The company operates on the principles of economic accounting, self-development and self-financing.

  • State licenses for construction, energy and trade
  • Own fleet of more than 100 machinery units
  • Collaboration experience with Sinopec in oil & gas
