Our services

Six fields — one responsible contractor. Full cycle: design, construction, state-commission handover.

Civil & commercial construction

We build administrative-commercial complexes, housing and social facilities — from foundations to facades and interior finishing. Delivered works include the “Digital Systems Center” in Ashgabat, a health house in Lebap velayat and apartment buildings for the Ministry of Defense in Tejen.

  • Administrative and commercial buildings
  • Apartment buildings and cottages
  • Social facilities: health houses, stores
  • Reconstruction and interior finishing
Digital Systems Center, Ashgabat

Power infrastructure

The company’s core specialization since 2009: construction and reconstruction of substations, transformer installation and power-line works — including 20 km of 35/10 kV overhead lines in the city of Serdar.

  • 35/10 kV and 110/35/10 kV substations
  • 0.4–10 kV overhead and cable power lines
  • Complete 10/0.4 kV transformer points
  • Reconstruction of existing networks

Water supply systems

Nine delivered water projects: main pipelines, pumping stations and reservoirs of up to 3,400 m³ — drinking water for villages, kindergartens and entire districts of Ahal velayat.

  • Main and distribution water pipelines
  • Pumping stations and reservoirs
  • External and internal building networks
  • Reconstruction of drinking-water systems

Industrial facilities

We build turnkey production facilities with all utilities and process lines: a confectionery and jam factory in Anew, a mayonnaise products plant and a wool yarn and blanket factory in the Ak Bugday district.

  • Food production plants
  • Textile factories
  • Warehouses and production workshops
  • Industrial utility networks
Factory in Anew

Roads & telecom

First-degree highways in Jebel (Balkan velayat) and Murche (Ahal velayat), and an automatic telephone station in the Choganly residential complex of Ashgabat.

  • First-degree highways
  • Road infrastructure and grounds
  • Automatic telephone stations
  • Utility communications
ATS, Choganly
